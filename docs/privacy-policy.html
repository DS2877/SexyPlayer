Aeria+ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Aeria+ is an IPTV player. It provides, hosts, and controls no channels, streams, or media of its own. You connect an IPTV service that you already subscribe to, and the app plays it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app remembers is kept on your Apple TV. None of it is sent to us — we do not operate servers that receive your data.

  • Provider credentials (server address, username, password, or a playlist URL) are stored in the device Keychain. They are used only to contact the provider you configured, and are never logged or transmitted anywhere else.
  • Your library is fetched from your provider and cached on the device so the app starts quickly. It stays on the device.
  • Watch progress, favourites, and preferences (languages, subtitle choice, which rows appear, etc.) are stored locally.

Network connections the app makes

  • Your IPTV provider — to load your channels/movies/series, the programme guide, and to stream what you play. This connection is between your Apple TV and your provider only.
  • AI-assisted search — only if you turn it on in Settings. When enabled, an ambiguous search sends the words you typed plus the list of genres and languages present in your library to an AI service, which returns a structured interpretation. It never sends your provider details, stream links, watch history, or any identifier. This setting is off by default.
  • Artwork & metadata — the app fills in posters, backdrops, synopses, ratings and cast from The Movie Database. Each request sends only a title and a year. It never sends any identifier, your provider details, or what you watch. This uses a key bundled with the app, or your own key if you add one in Settings.
  • Images are also loaded from any artwork URLs your provider supplies.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of any information — there is nothing collected to sell or share.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone. An optional "Hide adult categories" setting (on by default) filters adult-labelled content out of the interface.

Your control

Removing a provider deletes its stored credentials and cached library. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
