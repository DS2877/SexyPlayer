Getting started

On first launch, choose how you connect: Xtream Codes (server address, username, password) or an M3U playlist URL. Your details are stored securely on the Apple TV and used only to reach the provider you entered. There's also a Demo Library if you just want to look around.

Common questions

"Sign-in failed"
Double-check the server address (include http:// and the port, e.g. http://example.com:8080), the username, and the password. Some providers also cap the number of connections — make sure another device isn't using the slot.
What video formats play?
Aeria+ uses the native Apple TV player for HLS (.m3u8), MP4 and MOV, and a bundled decoder (VLCKit) for everything else your provider is likely to send — MKV, AVI, raw MPEG-TS (.ts), and RTMP/RTSP streams. If a specific stream still won't start, it's usually a dead link on the provider's side, a hit connection limit, or a geo-block.
A stream buffers or stalls
That's almost always the provider or your network, not the app. Try another channel to confirm; check your Apple TV's connection under Settings; and make sure another device isn't using your provider's connection slot.
The library is taking a long time to load
The first import of a very large library can take a minute or two. After that it's cached and starts quickly; it refreshes in the background. You can enter the app before it finishes.
Channel or movie names look messy
The app cleans up provider names automatically, but very unusual formatting can slip through. Let us know examples and we'll improve it.
There's no TV guide
The guide is built from EPG data. For Xtream that's automatic; for an M3U playlist you also need to enter an XMLTV / EPG URL when you add the provider (Settings → Your Provider → add again to update it). If your provider supplies no EPG, the guide stays empty and that's expected.
I only want Swedish / Nordic channels
Settings → Personalize → "Nordic & English only" (on by default) hides the flood of other-region channels and titles. Turn it off to see your provider's full catalogue.
How do I change my languages / which rows show on Home?
Settings → Personalize.
How do I remove or switch providers?
Settings → Your Provider.
Continue Watching won't clear an item
Press and hold the Siri Remote clickpad on any Continue Watching poster for Mark as Watched or Remove. History → Clear All wipes the lot.
